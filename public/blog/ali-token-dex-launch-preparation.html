ALI Token DEX Launch Preparation

PancakeSwap liquidity planning for a transparent charity token, without hype or price promises.

May 8, 2026

ALI Charity is preparing the public materials needed before ALI Token can be made tradable through a decentralized exchange liquidity pool.

The recommended first route is BNB Smart Chain and PancakeSwap, using an ALI/USDT pair. USDT keeps the trading narrative close to ALI Charity's donation accounting language, where 1 ALI is used as a 1 USDT donation reward reference.

What is ready now

  • ALI Token contract: 0x4de5F4ac5daC9667eD38A09B908B6Ee7D6E06E79
  • DEX preparation page: ALI Token DEX Launch Preparation
  • Risk disclosure covering volatility, liquidity provider risk, and the absence of profit guarantees.
  • A post-launch website update path once the wallet owner creates the pool and shares the pool link.

What still requires wallet control

Creating a liquidity pool is a real on-chain action. It requires a wallet signature and launch capital in both ALI and the quote token, such as USDT. Codex can prepare the website, announcement, checklist, and verification flow, but the wallet owner must confirm the transaction.

Responsible promotion rule

ALI Charity will promote the DEX launch as transparency infrastructure, not as a speculative price campaign. The focus is public contract verification, donor awareness, liquidity transparency, and clear risk education.
