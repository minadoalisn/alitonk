Editorial standards

Sources first

Factual claims link to primary sources such as the FTC, IRS and explorer documentation, with access dates.

Disclosed method

AI-assisted drafting is disclosed. Claims about ALI Charity are limited to what public records support.

No unsupported numbers

No donor counts, totals or impact figures without dated, reviewable evidence. Unknown does not mean zero.
