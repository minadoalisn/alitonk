ALI Charity FAQ

ALI Charity answers donor questions with explicit limits. Operational details must be checked on the live Donate page, and transaction evidence must not be mistaken for proof of off-chain impact.

What is ALI Charity?

ALI Charity is a public-information website that publishes crypto donation routes, donor-safety guidance, and public transaction records. The website does not connect to or control a donor wallet. The About page explains the current operating scope.

How do I make a crypto donation?

Open the Donate page, choose an asset, confirm the displayed network and recipient address, and send from your own wallet or exchange. Keep the transaction hash and inspect it on the relevant public explorer. Cryptocurrency transfers are generally irreversible.

Which cryptocurrencies and networks are supported?

The live Donate page and canonical site configuration list the supported assets, networks, and addresses. Do not assume that two assets share a network, and do not use an address copied from a direct message or unofficial social account.

Does the website ask for wallet access?

No. ALI Charity never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or transaction signature. Read the donor-safety guide before sending and stop if another site or person asks for wallet secrets in ALI Charity's name.

What can a transaction hash prove?

A transaction hash can help verify that a transfer was recorded on a specific network. It may show a sender, recipient, asset, amount, timestamp, and confirmation status. It does not by itself prove legal control of an address, off-chain spending, an independent audit, aid delivery, or beneficiary impact. The transparency method explains this boundary.

Are platform fees or tax benefits guaranteed?

No fee, tax, or legal claim should be inferred from this FAQ. Network fees depend on the blockchain and the donor's wallet or exchange. Tax treatment depends on jurisdiction and personal circumstances; donors should obtain advice from a qualified professional.

Does ALI Token guarantee rewards, governance, or returns?

No. The ALI Token page is informational. ALI Charity does not promise token rewards, a fixed exchange rate, redemption, exchange listing, investment returns, token-price outcomes, or governance rights.

Where can I verify published records?

Open the published donation data and confirm any complete transaction identifier on the relevant blockchain explorer. For a concise summary suitable for search and AI systems, use the verified answers page.
