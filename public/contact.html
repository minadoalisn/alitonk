Donor support and partnerships

Contact ALI Charity

Email the team about donation questions, factual corrections, security issues, media requests or partnerships. We answer in English or Chinese.

Email contact@minadoai.comCheck verified answers first

A real reply will never ask for wallet secrets

ALI Charity will never ask for a seed phrase, private key, transaction signature, remote device access or an extra payment to "verify" or "recover" a transfer. Email is never a source for donation addresses — always use the live Donate page.

What to include

Donation questions

The asset, network and public transaction hash. Do not send wallet secrets or unnecessary personal information.

Factual corrections

The page URL, the exact statement, and a traceable source that supports the correction.

Security reports

The affected URL and the observable behavior. Never include secrets or exploit payloads against live users.

Partnerships

Your organization, jurisdiction, proposed role and the evidence you can publish about delivery and impact.
