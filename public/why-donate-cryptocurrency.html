Example:

Common Questions About Crypto Donations

Q: Is donating cryptocurrency safe?

A: Crypto donations can be safe when donors verify the official URL, address, chain, and transaction hash. Blockchain transfers are irreversible, so donors should avoid private-message address changes and use a small test transfer when unsure.

Q: What cryptocurrencies do you accept?

A: ALI Charity donor pages currently describe BTC, ETH, BNB, USDT, and SOL donation options. USDT, ETH, and BNB use the published BNB Smart Chain address; BTC and SOL use separate native addresses.

Q: How do I track my donation?

A: Every donation generates a unique transaction ID that you can check on BSC Explorer. Our platform also provides a tracking dashboard.

Q: Can I donate anonymously?

A: Yes. Your donation transaction is public, but your identity can remain private. We respect donor privacy.

Q: What if I donate to a fraudulent cause?

A: Crypto transfers are generally irreversible. Before sending, verify the official minadoai.com page, chain, and address, and do not trust private messages that claim a donation address changed.

Q: How much of my donation actually reaches the cause?

A: At ALI Charity, 100% of your donation minus minimal blockchain gas fees reaches the cause. Platform fees = 0%.
