Three steps

How it works

1. Choose an asset

USDT, BNB, ETH, BTC or SOL. Each asset has exactly one official address and one network — see the live table on our Donate page.

2. Send from your wallet

Copy the address from our site, double-check the asset and the network, and send a small test transfer first if you are unsure. Crypto transfers are irreversible.

3. Verify on-chain

Keep your transaction hash and paste it into the relevant explorer — Etherscan, BscScan, Solscan or blockchain.com.

Addresses can change. Always copy from the live page — never from a screenshot, a forwarded message or a social post.

Public proof

How to verify your donation

  1. Open the explorer for your asset: Etherscan (ETH / ERC-20), BscScan (BNB Chain), Solscan (SOL), blockchain.com (BTC).
  2. Paste your transaction hash into the search bar.
  3. Confirm the recipient matches the official address published on our Donate page.

A public transfer record proves funds moved to an address. It does not, by itself, prove off-chain spending or beneficiary impact — read our transparency method to understand exactly what each record supports.

Questions

Giving Tuesday FAQ

Is it safe to donate crypto to ALI Charity?

Yes — because there is nothing to connect and nothing to sign on our site. You send from your own wallet. We never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and this website never signs a transaction.

Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?

USDT, BNB, ETH, BTC and SOL. Each has one official address and network, published on our Donate page.

How do I verify my donation arrived?

Keep your transaction hash and paste it into the blockchain explorer for that asset to confirm the transfer to our published address.

Is my crypto donation tax-deductible?

It depends on where you live. In the United States, the IRS treats donated cryptocurrency as property, which can make donating appreciated crypto tax-efficient. Rules vary by jurisdiction and we are not tax advisors — talk to yours.

What happens after I donate?

Your transfer appears in our public records with an explicit verification status. We do not inflate totals: unverified entries stay marked unverified, and unknown amounts stay unknown — not zero.
