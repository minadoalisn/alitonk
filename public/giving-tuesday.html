Addresses can change. Always copy from the live page — never from a screenshot, a forwarded message or a social post.

Public proof

How to verify your donation

  1. Select your asset on the Donate page and follow its explorer link. The current routes are USDT, BNB and ETH on BNB Smart Chain (BSC), BTC on Bitcoin, and SOL on Solana. ETH here is not Ethereum mainnet.
  2. Find your transaction using its hash on that network. Confirm its success and confirmation status; a pending or failed transaction is not confirmed receipt.
  3. Check the recipient against the selected published address, and check the exact asset or token, transferred amount and network. A matching address or hash alone is insufficient.

A public transfer record proves funds moved to an address. It does not, by itself, prove off-chain spending or beneficiary impact — read our transparency method to understand exactly what each record supports.

Questions

Giving Tuesday FAQ

Is it safe to donate crypto to ALI Charity?

Not connecting a wallet is not a safety guarantee. This site does not request wallet access, signatures, seed phrases or private keys. Transfers can still be irreversible or sent to the wrong recipient or network. If you cannot verify the recipient and intended use, do not send.

Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?

Published options are USDT, BNB and ETH on BNB Smart Chain (BSC), BTC on Bitcoin, and SOL on Solana. ETH here is not Ethereum mainnet. Check the selected asset and network on the Donate page before sending.

How do I verify my donation arrived?

Select the asset on the Donate page and use its network-specific explorer link. BscScan covers this site's BSC options (USDT, BNB and ETH); Bitcoin and Solana use their own explorers. Check the transaction's success and confirmation status, recipient, asset or token, amount and network. A hash alone does not establish receipt or charitable use.

Is my crypto donation tax-deductible?

ALI Charity's eligibility to receive tax-deductible contributions is unverified; supporting registration evidence has not been published here. Do not assume a deduction. US donors should check IRS qualified-organization rules and the organization's eligibility; other jurisdictions have different rules. Consult a qualified tax adviser.

What happens after I donate?

This static site does not automatically record transfers, verify receipt or issue a tax receipt. A transfer may be absent from the published records. Unverified entries remain unverified; unknown totals remain unknown, not zero. Review our transparency method.

Is USDT safe to donate under U.S. regulation?

U.S. stablecoin regulation reached a milestone in 2026: the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency issued its final rule implementing the GENIUS Act on October 21, 2026, effective January 18, 2027. The rule requires issuers under OCC jurisdiction to hold reserves worth at least as much as their outstanding stablecoins and generally process redemptions within two business days. That framework governs the asset, not this charity: it does not make ALI Charity itself regulated, and it does not change the tax treatment of a donation.
