Why ALI / USDT First

Clear charity accounting

USDT is easier for donors to understand because ALI Charity already communicates a fixed 1 ALI = 1 USDT donation accounting rate.

BSC-native access

BNB Smart Chain users can inspect the ALI contract on BscScan and interact with PancakeSwap without relying on a centralized exchange listing.

Transparent launch trail

The pool creation transaction, liquidity position, and future swap activity can be linked publicly after the wallet owner signs the launch transaction.

Wallet Signing Checklist

  1. Open the official PancakeSwap liquidity page.
  2. Connect the wallet that controls the launch funds.
  3. Select BNB Smart Chain.
  4. Import ALI by contract address: 0x4de5F4ac5daC9667eD38A09B908B6Ee7D6E06E79.
  5. Select USDT as the quote asset.
  6. Choose PancakeSwap V3 unless a V2 pool is intentionally preferred.
  7. Set fee tier and price range carefully; avoid an extreme range that makes the pool inactive.
  8. Enter the ALI and USDT amounts, review the implied opening price, then approve token spending.
  9. Confirm the add-liquidity transaction in the wallet.
  10. Copy the pool or position link and send it back to Codex so the website can be updated with the live trading link.

Suggested Launch Inputs

  • Base token: ALI, contract 0x4de5F4ac5daC9667eD38A09B908B6Ee7D6E06E79.
  • Quote token: USDT on BNB Smart Chain.
  • Pair name: ALI/USDT.
  • Initial price: choose manually in PancakeSwap based on the wallet owner's liquidity plan.
  • Gas reserve: keep enough BNB in the wallet for approval and add-liquidity transactions.
  • After signing: keep the transaction hash, pool address, and PancakeSwap link for public verification.

Risk Disclosure

  • ALI is not presented as a guaranteed investment, yield product, or promise of profit.
  • DEX liquidity can be volatile, thin, and vulnerable to large price swings.
  • Liquidity providers face impermanent loss and smart contract risk.
  • Only the wallet owner should sign transactions. Never paste private keys or seed phrases into any website or chat.
  • Marketing should emphasize charity transparency and utility, not price appreciation.
