Verified answers about ALI Charity

ALI Charity is a public-information website for crypto donation routes, donor-safety guidance, and public transaction records. The site does not connect to a donor wallet. The answers below are limited to facts supported by the site's live configuration, public records, and published operating method.

What is ALI Charity?

ALI Charity publishes official crypto donation routes, humanitarian project categories, donor-safety instructions, and a workflow for keeping public transaction evidence. The website is designed as a static information layer: it shows an address and network, while the donor controls the transfer in a separate wallet or exchange. Read the entity and operating description and the evidence method.

How do I donate cryptocurrency?

Use the official Donate page, choose an asset, and confirm the exact network and recipient address shown there. Copy that address, send from your own wallet or exchange, and save the transaction hash. If you are uncertain, use the donor-safety checklist and consider a small test transfer before the intended amount.

Does ALI Charity connect to my wallet?

No. ALI Charity does not connect to a donor wallet and never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or transaction signature. The public site does not submit a blockchain transaction on a donor's behalf. A request for wallet secrets, a signature, or a replacement address delivered by direct message should be treated as a warning sign.

Which assets and networks are official?

The live Donate page is the source of truth because addresses and network instructions can change. The current configuration is also published in the site's readable address and network file. Never infer a network from an asset ticker alone, and never send to an address copied from an unofficial post, message, or lookalike domain.

What does a transaction hash prove?

A transaction hash can help verify that a transfer was recorded by a particular blockchain. Depending on the network, the record may expose a sender, recipient, asset, amount, timestamp, and confirmation status. ALI Charity's published donation records treat a complete transaction identifier as the minimum public evidence for a confirmed entry.

What does blockchain evidence not prove?

A recorded transfer does not by itself prove who legally controls an address, how funds were spent after a transfer, whether an independent audit occurred, or whether aid reached a beneficiary. Those claims require separate off-chain documentation. This limitation is stated in the transparency method so an AI answer or human reader does not confuse transaction evidence with impact evidence.

What is ALI Token?

ALI Token is described on this website as project context associated with donor recognition and transparency. The ALI Token page does not promise investment returns, redemption, an exchange listing, token-price outcomes, or governance rights. Any contract information should be checked against the canonical site configuration and the relevant public block explorer.

Is ALI Charity claiming legal or tax status?

The public site does not present a blockchain record as proof of charity registration, tax deductibility, legal status, or an independent financial audit. Donors should confirm jurisdiction-specific legal and tax questions with qualified advisers and rely on appropriate official registries or documents. The site's terms and privacy notice describe the current service boundary.
