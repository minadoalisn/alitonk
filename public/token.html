ALI Token: what it is, and what it is not.

ALI is a BEP-20 token that ALI Charity describes as a donor-recognition and transparency layer. It is not an investment product, and you do not need it to donate.

Risk disclosure. ALI Charity does not promise a reward rate, redemption value, stable market price, exchange listing, investment return or governance right. Never buy a token because someone promises profit.

Intended purpose

Donor recognition

ALI can act as a public participation record for donors who want their support connected to a transparent campaign history.

Future governance (planned)

Governance remains an experiment. Any voting or campaign-selection feature would be introduced only with published rules and documentation.

DEX readiness

No active DEX listing, liquidity level or trading outcome is claimed. A future pool must be linked from minadoai.com and verified on a public explorer.

Due diligence checklist

Before donating

  • Open the official Donate page on minadoai.com.
  • Match the asset and network exactly.
  • Copy the address from the live page, never from chats or screenshots.
  • Keep the transaction hash.

Before any token interaction

  • Confirm the contract address above on BscScan.
  • Wait for an official pool link published on this site.
  • Understand slippage, liquidity and smart-contract risk.
  • Treat ALI as having no guaranteed value.

Common questions

What is the official ALI Token contract address?

The ALI Token contract on BNB Smart Chain (BSC) is 0x4de5F4ac5daC9667eD38A09B908B6Ee7D6E06E79 with 18 decimals. Verify it on BscScan and on minadoai.com; ignore contract addresses sent in private messages.

Is ALI Token an investment?

No. ALI Charity presents ALI only as donor-recognition and transparency context. It does not promise a return, reward rate, redemption value, stable price, exchange listing or governance right.

Is ALI Token listed on a DEX such as PancakeSwap?

No active DEX listing or liquidity pool is claimed on this page. Any future pool must be announced on minadoai.com and verified on a public explorer before anyone interacts with it.

Do I need ALI Token to donate?

No. Donations use USDT, BNB or ETH on BNB Smart Chain, BTC on Bitcoin, or SOL on Solana, sent from your own wallet to the addresses on the official Donate page.
