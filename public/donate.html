Donation FAQ

Is it safe to donate crypto to ALI Charity?

Yes — because there is nothing to connect and nothing to sign on our site. You send from your own wallet. We never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and this website never signs a transaction.

Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?

USDT, BNB, ETH, BTC and SOL. Each has one official address and network, shown in Step 1 above when you choose an asset.

How do I verify my donation arrived?

Keep your transaction hash and paste it into the blockchain explorer for that asset — Etherscan, BscScan, Solscan or blockchain.com — to confirm the transfer to our published address.

Is my crypto donation tax-deductible?

It depends on where you live. In the United States, the IRS treats donated cryptocurrency as property, which can make donating appreciated crypto tax-efficient. Rules vary by jurisdiction and we are not tax advisors — talk to yours.

What happens after I donate?

Your transfer appears in our public records with an explicit verification status. We do not inflate totals: unverified entries stay marked unverified, and unknown amounts stay unknown — not zero. See our transparency method.
