About ALI Charity

ALI Charity is a crypto donation information platform that publishes official donation routes, donor-safety instructions, humanitarian project categories, and a workflow for retaining public transaction records. A blockchain record can verify a transfer; it does not by itself prove off-chain delivery, legal status, an independent audit, or beneficiary impact.

Last reviewed: August 17, 2026 · Maintained by: ALI Charity Operations · Method: entity facts are checked against the Donate page and canonical site configuration. AI-assisted maintenance is disclosed; factual claims require source review.

Our Purpose

ALI Charity helps a donor verify the official domain, asset, chain, recipient address, and transaction-hash workflow before sending an irreversible crypto transfer.

The site does not connect to or control a donor's wallet. Donors send from their own wallet or exchange and should keep the transaction hash. Public transaction records are the first evidence layer; project delivery, expenditure, and beneficiary claims require separate off-chain documentation.

What Is Not Currently Claimed

This public About page does not claim that ALI Charity is the world's first blockchain charity, that thousands of donors have participated, or that every project expenditure and beneficiary outcome has been independently verified.

The public site does not currently present an independent audit, third-party endorsement, or verified off-chain impact bundle as proof for those statements. If that evidence becomes available, it should be published with dates, responsible parties, source documents, and a clear review status.
