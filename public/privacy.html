Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026Applies to: minadoai.com

Summary

This is a static information website. It never connects to your wallet, never asks for a seed phrase or private key, sets no advertising cookies and does not sell personal data. Aggregate, cookieless analytics help us measure page performance and which pages are useful.

1. What this website does not collect

  • No wallet connection, signatures, seed phrases or private keys — the site has no wallet integration.
  • No account registration, donation forms or payment card data.
  • No advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers.

2. Data processed when you visit

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, time) to deliver pages, protect against abuse and diagnose errors.

Aggregate analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights, which do not use cookies and report aggregated page views and performance metrics. We also use a GEOInflow script (served from geoinflow-iota.vercel.app) to understand aggregate referral sources, including visits from AI search assistants. We use this information only to improve content and performance.

Local browser checks

Pages read the public donation-address configuration and the public records file from this domain. Copying an address uses your browser's clipboard locally; nothing about the copy is sent to us.

3. Information you send us

If you email contact@minadoai.com, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and keep a record of the request. Do not send wallet secrets or unnecessary personal information.

4. Public blockchain data

Crypto transfers are recorded on public blockchains by design. Anyone can see the sending address, receiving address, amount and time of a transfer on an explorer. ALI Charity cannot hide, edit or delete that data, and it may publish transaction identifiers in its public records file under the transparency method.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers described above to operate the site, or where required by law.

6. Retention

Hosting and analytics data are retained according to the providers' policies. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal information. Contact contact@minadoai.com to make a request.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We will update this page, including the date above, before changing what data the site collects.
