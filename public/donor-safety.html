What A Transaction Hash Proves — And What It Does Not

A transaction hash can help verify

  • That a transaction was recorded on the selected public blockchain.
  • The sender and recipient addresses visible to that network.
  • The asset, amount, timestamp, and confirmation status shown by the explorer.

A transaction hash does not prove by itself

  • Who legally controls the recipient address.
  • That off-chain procurement or aid delivery occurred.
  • Beneficiary counts, charity registration, an independent audit, or third-party endorsement.

Source basis: the U.S. Federal Trade Commission explains that cryptocurrency payments are typically irreversible and commonly recorded on a public blockchain. ALI Charity applies the narrower rule that a hash proves the recorded transfer, not the off-chain impact.

Quick Verification Flow

1. Confirm the source

Type minadoai.com directly, avoid shortened links, and ignore private messages that claim a new donation address.

2. Confirm the chain and address

USDT, ETH, and BNB donations use the BNB Smart Chain address shown on the official Donate page. BTC and SOL use separate native addresses.

Before sending, follow the full-address check below.

3. Keep the proof

Save the transaction hash, amount, asset, recipient address, timestamp, and a copy of the donation page used at the time of transfer.

Check the whole address, not just the ends

Open the official Donate page, confirm the asset and network, and compare the entire recipient address in your wallet with the address on that page, including every middle character. Matching only the first and last characters is not enough. Never copy a recipient from transaction history. Stop if the source, address or network is uncertain.

An exact address match only checks consistency with this site's published instructions. It does not verify who controls the wallet or prove a charitable use of funds.

Red flags before sending

  • A social account or direct message gives a different address.
  • The page promises token price gains, refunds, doubled donations, or guaranteed returns.
  • The chain does not match the asset you selected.
  • You cannot find a block explorer record after the transfer confirms.
