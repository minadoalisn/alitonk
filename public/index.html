Quick answers

Before you give.

What is ALI Charity?

ALI Charity is a static information website at minadoai.com that publishes official crypto donation addresses, donor-safety guidance and public transaction records for humanitarian project routes. It never connects to a donor's wallet.

Which cryptocurrencies can I donate?

USDT, BNB and ETH on BNB Smart Chain (BSC), BTC on the Bitcoin network, and SOL on Solana. Each asset must be sent only on the network shown on the official Donate page.

Does ALI Charity need access to my wallet?

No. You send from your own wallet or exchange. ALI Charity never asks for a wallet connection, a transaction signature, a seed phrase or a private key.

Are donations to ALI Charity tax-deductible?

ALI Charity's tax-deductible status is unverified: no registration evidence is published and the site does not issue tax receipts. More in verified answers.
